Brain wave frequency generator

Pick a band and beat frequency to hear continuous, steady binaural tones generated live in your browser. Playback has no automatic time limit and continues until you press Stop; no intentional on/off pulses or automatic frequency sweeps are added. Nothing is downloaded or recorded.

Use stereo headphones. A binaural beat needs a different tone in each ear; through speakers the two tones mix in the air and the effect is lost. Keep the volume low and comfortable, and don’t listen while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. Binaural beats are an audio experience, not a proven treatment, and they do not reliably change brain activity, sleep, focus, meditation or memory. EEG band boundaries are approximate, can overlap, and differ between sources (some put theta at 4–7 Hz or alpha at 8–12 Hz, for example), and the bands are not guaranteed mental states. Nothing here claims to entrain brain waves.

Testing instructions and self-test
  1. Press Run self-test. It renders every band at its lowest, middle and highest beat frequency offline in this browser, measures each channel’s frequency, and checks left = carrier and right = carrier + beat. The Node test suite (npm test) runs the same check plus lifecycle tests.
  2. By ear: wear stereo headphones, play Alpha at 10 Hz with a 200 Hz carrier, and cover one ear. You should hear one steady tone; with both ears you should hear a slow pulsing.
  3. Channel check: the readouts above show the exact left and right frequencies. Swap headphones left/right and the tone you hear in each ear should swap.
  4. Cleanup: start playback, then switch bands, press Stop, or close the tab. Sound fades out and stops each time. In Chrome you can confirm in chrome://media-internals that the audio context is closed.
Results appear here.

Everything runs locally with the Web Audio API: two sine oscillators, one per channel, joined through a channel merger so left and right stay separate. Frequencies below about 20 Hz aren’t heard as tones, so each beat is made from two audible carrier tones whose difference equals the target frequency.
